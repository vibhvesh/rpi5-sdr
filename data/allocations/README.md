# Spectrum allocation tags

These JSON files are **approximate labels** used only to tag detections
(for example "FM broadcasting" or "Amateur 2 m").

They are **not**:
- official copies of India’s NFAP, the FCC allocation table, or any other
  administration’s frequency table
- legal advice
- a claim that a service is authorized, unencrypted, or legal to listen to
  at your location

Service names are simplified and may be outdated or incomplete. Always check
the current national table (WPC/NFAP in India, FCC in the US, etc.) and local
law before receiving. This software never decodes cellular payloads, trunked
public-safety systems, or encrypted channels.
